## Intended household scope

- one or two Russian-citizen adults;
- exactly two children aged 6–17;
- ordinary Russian foreign-travel passports;
- travel originating in Moscow or Saint Petersburg;
- a stay of at least 30 calendar days.

## Initial extracted conclusion

The official material appeared to describe an ordinary visa-free visit of up to
30 days for holders of ordinary Russian passports, general border conditions,
and host registration within 24 hours. Each component is now modeled as a
separate structured claim. It is supported only when its current automated proof
packet passes the complete publication policy.
